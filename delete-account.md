# Delete your account and data

This page explains how to ask us to delete your Astalakshmi Jewellers account and the personal information held with it.

## Read this before you request deletion

**Deleting your account forfeits any gold left in it.**

Your gold holding exists as a record against your account. If the account is deleted, that record is deleted with it, and the gold cannot be recovered or returned afterwards - not as metal, and not as money.

**So redeem your gold first.** Any balance can be collected as physical jewellery at our store, in person. There is no time limit of redemption and no charge for holding it, so there is no reason to rush a deletion request.

If you want to close your account without collecting your gold, we can do that - but only on your explicit written instruction confirming that you understand the balance will be lost. We will not act on an ambiguous request.

## How to request deletion

**Step 1 - Redeem your balance.** Visit our store at **No 121, Lb road, Adyar, chennai - 600020** during business hours and collect your gold as jewellery.

**Step 2 - Email us.** Write to **info.astalakshmijewellers@gmail.com** with the subject line **"Delete my account"**, including your registered mobile number so we can identify the account.

**Step 3 - Confirm.** We will reply telling you the exact gold balance currently in your account. If any balance remains, we will not proceed until you reply confirming, in writing, that you want the account deleted and accept that the balance will be lost.

We may also ask you to verify your identity before acting, so that nobody else can delete your account or give up your gold.

We will respond to the initial request within **7 business days**, and complete the deletion once your confirmation is received.

## What gets deleted

- Your name and mobile number
- Your login credentials
- Your account record

## What we have to keep, and why

Your purchase and redemption history is a **financial record**. Indian tax, accounting and anti-money-laundering law require us to retain these for a defined period, so we cannot delete them on request while that obligation applies. Retained records are kept only for that purpose and are not used to contact you or to market anything.
