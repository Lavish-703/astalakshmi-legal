Privacy Policy

Last Updated: [18/11/2026]

This policy explains what personal information the Astalakshmi Jewellers app collects, why, and what we do with it. It applies to the App operated by Astalakshmi Jewellers, registered at No 121, Lb road, Adyar, chennai - 600020.

We have tried to describe only what we actually do, rather than everything we might conceivably do.

1. What we collect

Information you give us: your name and mobile number at registration, and a PIN you choose. That is the whole of the profile information we ask for.

We do not collect your email address, date of birth, address, PAN, Aadhaar, photograph, contacts, location, or anything from your camera, microphone or files.

Information created by using the App: your gold holding in grams, and your purchase and redemption history — the quantity, the rate applied, the amount, the date and the outcome, plus a payment reference provided by the gateway.

Technical information: basic device and connection details sent when the App talks to our servers, and records of payment notifications we receive from the gateway. We keep these to investigate failed payments and to detect misuse.

2. What we do not have

We never receive your payment credentials. Card numbers, CVV, UPI PIN, net-banking passwords and one-time codes for payment all go directly to PhonePe and your bank. They do not pass through our systems and we could not disclose them if we wanted to.

We do not collect any special category data — nothing about health, religion, caste, biometrics or political views.

3. Why we use it

- To create and operate your account, and to log you in

- To price, place and settle your gold purchases

- To maintain an accurate record of your holding

- To verify your identity when you collect gold at our store

- To investigate failed or disputed payments

- To detect and prevent fraud and unauthorised access

- To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not build marketing profiles, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

4. Who we share it with

- PhonePe, our payment gateway, to process your payments. PhonePe receives the order reference and amount, and handles your payment details directly under its own privacy policy.

- Service providers that make the App work — hosting, databases, SMS delivery for one-time passwords, and similar. They act on our instructions and may use your information only to provide that service.

- Our store staff, who see your name and holding when you come in to collect gold.

- Authorities and advisers, where the law requires it or to establish or defend a legal claim.

Some of these services are operated by third parties we do not control. We select them with reasonable care, but we are not responsible for their own acts, omissions or outages, and your dealings with a third party may also be governed by that party’s terms and privacy policy.

5. How we protect it

Connections between the App and our servers are encrypted in transit. Your login session is held in your device’s secure storage, not in ordinary app storage. Your PIN is stored in a form designed so that it cannot be read back. [CONFIRM PIN STORAGE WORDING]

Access to customer records inside our organisation is limited to people who need it to do their job. Every change to an order or holding is written to an internal audit record.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise absolute security. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account and holding records for as long as your account is open, and afterwards for as long as we are required to by tax, accounting and anti-money-laundering law, or for as long as needed to resolve a dispute.

Transaction and payment records are financial records. We cannot delete them on request while a retention obligation applies.

Technical and diagnostic records are kept for a shorter period and routinely cleared.

7. Your choices

You can view your profile and your full transaction history in the App at any time.

You can ask us to correct your name or mobile number, ask what information we hold about you, or ask us to close your account. Write to info.astalakshmijewellers@gmail.com and we will respond within 7 business days.

Two honest limits. Closing your account does not erase transaction records we must keep by law. And redeem your gold before asking us to close your account.

8. Children

The App is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has registered, tell us and we will remove the account.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always in the App, with the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use your information, we will notify you in the App before it takes effect.
